# Writing Beats

Use only when the user explicitly asks to shape fragments into beats.

Read the supplied material first. Agree on the piece's goal and any reader prerequisites. A beat makes one move—sets a scene, lands a point, asks a question or shifts the angle—and stops where the next move can begin. Each beat may use only ideas already familiar to the reader or grounded in an earlier beat. Mark any missing evidence as a gap instead of inventing it.

Arrange, split or combine fragments into a candidate sequence. Leave unused material in the source pile. Present the sequence for the user's review before drafting full prose. This is the optional middle step in the Fragments → Beats → Shape workflow.
