# Brand Foundation

Create or refine reusable brand knowledge from user intent and evidence.

Cover what is applicable:
- purpose, mission, vision;
- target audience and positioning;
- values and personality;
- brand principles;
- verbal tone;
- visual direction;
- constraints and avoid-list.

Do not start from a logo alone. Brand identity follows the foundation.

Persist a compact BRAND_PROFILE.yaml for agents and deeper human-readable brand documents for people. Reuse existing Product Manager/Product Designer roles unless repeated evidence proves a distinct role is needed.
