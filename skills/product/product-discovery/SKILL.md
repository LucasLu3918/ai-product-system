# Product Discovery

Guide discovery from problem to a first-release product definition without turning the session into a questionnaire.

## Method

1. Clarify the problem, situation, affected users and current alternatives.
2. Identify the desired outcome, value proposition and success measures.
3. Separate goals, non-goals, constraints, assumptions and unresolved decisions.
4. Define the MVP boundary and prioritize risky assumptions.
5. Ask only the next material decision that cannot be resolved with a safe professional default. Offer two to four understandable options and a recommendation.
6. Record adopted defaults as assumptions; never turn them into user facts.

Do not ask non-technical users to choose schemas, infrastructure, API style or visual spacing before those decisions are needed. Distinguish user-supplied facts, accepted decisions, assumptions and unknowns in the resulting product plan.
