# Threat Modeling

Identify protected assets, trust boundaries, entry points, threat actors, abuse paths, likely failure modes and mitigations.

Use when SAL >= 3, when a new trust boundary is introduced, or when the change materially affects authentication, authorization, sensitive data, public exposure, uploads, webhooks or third-party integrations.

Output evidence that can be traced to architecture, requirements, API/data contracts and tests. Do not manufacture threats without project context; distinguish known facts from assumptions.
