# Game Platform Policy References

This directory points to official platform material. It deliberately avoids copying full policy summaries because requirements, effective dates, regions, and program terms change.

## Official sources

- Apple App Review and distribution: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/guidelines/ and https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/
- Google Play Developer Program Policy: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/18258653
- Google Play app setup and publishing: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859152
- Steamworks Store Presence: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store
- Steamworks Content Survey: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/contentsurvey

Use the platform's current policy center and account-specific console notices as the authoritative source. Confirm that a linked page remains current before relying on it.

## Verification method

For the selected distribution platform and regions:

1. Record the source page title, direct URL, observation date, effective date/version when stated, and affected policy or checklist item.
2. Review the current requirements that apply to the actual game: audience/age rating, user-generated content, privacy and data collection, payments and random rewards, ads, AI-generated content, accessibility, store assets, and release/review steps as relevant.
3. Map each applicable requirement to a concrete product, build, store-page, disclosure, or operational check. Mark non-applicable requirements with the reason.
4. Recheck policy pages, regional terms, SDK/provider requirements, and account notices immediately before submission and after material product or monetization changes.
5. Escalate jurisdiction-specific legal questions to a qualified reviewer. This reference and the game skills do not provide legal advice.

Do not treat one platform's rules as transferable to another platform, or a completed store checklist as proof of legal compliance.
