# Commerce operations prompts

For operator workflows, define permissions, audit history, exception queues, and safe correction paths. For fulfillment, clarify carrier or warehouse ownership, shipment split, tracking, delivery exception, and customer notification behavior when in scope.

For support, identify the minimum order context needed and what must be masked. Define operational measures with a population, unit, time window, and target instead of vague goals. Record service boundaries, ownership, alerts, reconciliation tasks, retention assumptions, and open decisions.
