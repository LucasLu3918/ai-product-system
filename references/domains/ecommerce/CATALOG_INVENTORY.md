# Catalog and inventory prompts

## Catalog

Define product versus sellable variant, attributes, media, category, localization, publication state, and who may change each field. State whether products are physical, digital, configurable, or mixed.

## Availability

Name the inventory source of truth and whether availability is informational, reserved, or guaranteed. Clarify location, channel, back-order, preorder, stock adjustment, and concurrent purchase behavior when in scope.

## IDs to trace

Use stable domain IDs such as `DOM-PRODUCT`, `DOM-VARIANT`, and `DOM-INVENTORY`; use operation IDs such as `OP-CATALOG-SEARCH` only when those concepts or operations are actually in scope. These examples are prompts, not mandatory schema names.
