# Brand Governance

## Authority

BRAND_PROFILE.yaml is the fast index. Human-readable brand documents provide deeper authority.

## Permanent Change

A permanent change to positioning, core values, logo rules, primary visual identity or voice requires explicit user approval and corresponding updates across the Brand System.

## Temporary Override

A campaign/artifact may temporarily deviate when explicitly approved. Record scope, reason, allowed changes and protected rules. Do not silently promote the override into permanent brand policy.

## Consistency Review

Material branded artifacts should be checked against:
- brand purpose/personality;
- logo rules;
- color/type/image rules;
- verbal identity;
- must-use / must-avoid constraints.
